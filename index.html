YouTube Uploader

YouTube Uploader is a private, personal tool that uploads videos to the owner's own YouTube channels using the YouTube Data API. It is not offered to the public and is not intended for use by anyone other than its owner.

What it does

  • Uploads video files from the owner's computer to the owner's YouTube channels.
  • Sets each video's title, description, tags, privacy, schedule, thumbnail and playlist.
  • Reads basic channel information (channel name) to confirm which channel is connected.

It does not read, collect or share any other YouTube or Google account data.
