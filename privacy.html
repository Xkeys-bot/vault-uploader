Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

This policy covers Vault Uploader, a private tool used only by its owner to upload videos to the owner's own YouTube channels.

Information accessed

When the owner signs in with Google, the tool requests permission to upload and manage videos on the selected YouTube channel and to read that channel's basic information (name and ID). It accesses only what is needed to upload videos and confirm the connected channel.

How information is used and stored

  • Google sign-in tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer and are used only to upload the owner's videos.
  • No data is sent to any server other than Google's and YouTube's APIs.
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising or analytics.

Google API Services

Vault Uploader's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. It uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at Google Account permissions. Deleting the stored token files from the owner's computer also removes the tool's access.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue on GitHub.
